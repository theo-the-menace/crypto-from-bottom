# Glossary

- **Ask:** lowest price at which a seller is currently willing to sell.
- **Bid:** highest price at which a buyer is currently willing to buy.
- **CEX:** centralized exchange; a company operates custody, matching and account access.
- **Divergence:** price and an indicator move in different directions; a warning to investigate, not a guaranteed reversal.
- **Drawdown:** decline from an equity peak to a later trough.
- **Funding:** periodic payment between perpetual-futures longs and shorts.
- **K-line/candlestick:** OHLC summary for one time interval.
- **Leverage:** exposure controlled with borrowed or reserved collateral; it magnifies losses as well as gains.
- **Liquidity:** how much can be traded without moving price significantly.
- **Mark price:** reference price used by a derivatives venue for risk calculations under its contract rules.
- **OHLC:** open, high, low, close.
- **Perpetual:** derivative contract without a normal expiry date.
- **Spot:** buying or selling the underlying asset itself.
- **Spread:** ask minus bid.
- **Stop:** a predefined invalidation or exit condition; a stop-limit can still fail to fill.
